Clinical dictation without sending your work to the cloud.

MEHdictation is fast Windows voice dictation built around a simple default: Work / Offline. Audio and dictated text stay on your computer, with Comprehensive Medical vocabulary tuned for UK clinical language.

Install, choose your shortcut, dictate.

MEHdictation is currently an independent Windows build and may be treated cautiously by endpoint-security software because it is not yet code-signed. Do not bypass organisational security controls on a managed device.

01 · Install

Use Full Offline for a new PC

The local model is included, so Work / Offline does not need a cloud speech service.

02 · Configure

Pick a push-to-talk shortcut

Keyboard shortcuts use the safer Windows/Electron route. Mouse-button triggers are available where needed.

03 · Speak

Keep Work / Offline for clinical use

Comprehensive Medical vocabulary is selected by default and can be changed in Settings.

Clinical safety: MEHdictation is a dictation aid, not a medical device or clinical decision-support system. Review dictated text before it becomes part of a patient record, particularly names, drugs, doses, numbers, negation and laterality.
